Which CUET subjects does your course need?

Pick a university, then type your course, like “bcom program” or “B.A. Hons Economics”. You’ll see the subject combinations to choose in CUET.

Try: bcom program · bms · psychology · physics · german · bca

DU: what are List A and List B?

List A (languages): Assamese, Bengali, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu.

List B (domain subjects):

  1. Accountancy / Book Keeping
  2. Agriculture
  3. Anthropology
  4. Biology / Biological Science / Biotechnology / Biochemistry
  5. Business Studies
  6. Chemistry
  7. Computer Science / Information Practices
  8. Economics / Business Economics
  9. Environmental Studies / Environmental Science
  10. Fine Arts / Visual Arts / Commercial Arts
  11. Geography / Geology
  12. History
  13. Home Science
  14. Knowledge Tradition and Practices in India
  15. Mass Media / Mass Communication
  16. Mathematics / Applied Mathematics
  17. Performing Arts (Dance, Drama, Music)
  18. Physical Education (Yoga, Sports)
  19. Physics
  20. Political Science
  21. Psychology
  22. Sociology

You must appear in CUET in the subjects you studied or are studying in Class XII. Check the bulletin for details.

Check before you register. DU data comes from the official DU Bulletin of Information 2026–27. BHU, Allahabad and JNU data comes from the CUET-UG 2026 mapping as summarised by Physics Wallah, not from the official 2027 notification. Rules can change for 2027, so always confirm with each university’s latest bulletin.
